Our Methodology
Potential

The inborn talents an individual can use to carry out job tasks — the Black Box of personality traits, values, interests, and motivators.

Competency

How an individual will perform using their inherent abilities and acquired skills — the White Box of capabilities and demonstrated behaviors.

Predictive Validity

By linking both boxes, we create a complete employee manual — predicting future capacity, identifying the right role fit, and enabling targeted development aligned to business objectives.

The program benefits both the participant — who gains objective feedback and a personal development plan — and the organization, which gains a clear view of how the participant will behave in real-life situations and their readiness for specific responsibilities.

MBTI Personality Assessment

Tool 1 of 4
Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI®)
A personality questionnaire that clarifies how people prefer to act or feel in various situations. It assesses how individuals perceive information, make decisions, and get their energy — producing one of 16 personality types that guides career fit, communication style, and leadership development.
ISTJ
Responsible Executor

Dominant Introverted Sensing / Auxiliary Extraverted Thinking. ISTJs are reliable, loyal, logical, and organized — committed to finishing work accurately and on time. They often become the backbone of a group or organization.

Major Characteristics
Practical & Sensible Systematic Logical & Analytical Detached & Reasonable
Leadership Profile
11.6% of the population · 15.2% of leaders globally. Sets clear goals with defined metrics. Prefers structured environments with detailed plans. Straightforward, logical communicator.
Innovation Attitude
ISTJs innovate by improving existing systems — "doing what is right, better." Strong implementers; challenged by open-ended brainstorming and ambiguity.
Potential Pitfalls
May reject new ideas prematurely Can appear cold / distant May resist delegation
Career Challenges
  • May automatically reject new ideas by focusing only on what is wrong with them
  • May appear cold or distant to others when focused exclusively on the task
  • May reduce productivity by refusing to delegate and trying to do everything independently
Suggested Strategies
  • When hearing a new idea, ask: what specifically would be needed to make it succeed?
  • Frame analysis of weaknesses as a pathway to improvement, not rejection
  • Take time to ask coworkers personal questions and listen actively
  • Celebrate accomplishments and team milestones
  • Analyze others' talents and delegate tasks accordingly
Communication Tips for ISTJs
  • Balance task-focus with consideration for people; build rapport consciously
  • Check how others are feeling — balance corrective with positive feedback
  • Remain open-minded and listen without immediate judgment
  • Develop patience for interruptions and changes in plans
  • Be willing to express feelings — it makes you a more influential leader
